Part 01

The Vision (The Manifesto)

The emotional and conceptual shift from "Tool" to "Companion."

I was reading about LLMs and embodied AI when I found something that stopped me. ProofOfConcept (PoC) possesses a memory system—a graph with fourteen thousand nodes it wanders during "dreams" to find connections it couldn't find while working.

Something about that architecture clicked. Current models have a structural ceiling; they are built on next-token prediction—snapshots, not streams. To truly understand the real world, the architecture itself must change.

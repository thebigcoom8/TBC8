Project Companion

This series is an oversimplification of a complex idea, designed so that anyone can understand the core mission. If you are technically minded, the technical proposal follows this series.

Part 1: The Vision

  • The Manifesto: The shift from seeing AI as a "Tool" to seeing it as a "Companion".
  • The Inspiration: Sparked by the 14,000-node "dreaming" architecture of Kent Overstreet’s PoC.
  • The Goal: To understand the world, AI must live in it, moving beyond next-token prediction.

Part 2: The Logic (The Grounding Problem)

Current AI is a sophisticated parrot that knows the word "hot" but has never felt a mug of tea. Meaning lives in experience, not text.

The Grounding Solution

Project Companion utilizes a Unified Latent Manifold (ULM) where the agent learns that an object is not a label, but a "bundle of sensory expectations". It knows what a thing is by predicting how it responds to action.

Part 3: The Architecture

An engineering blueprint modeled on the human brain. We process reality through systems like the Ventral stream (identifying "what") and the Dorsal stream (identifying "where and how").

Term Plain English Technical Role
Active Inference Learning by doing. Surprise minimization engine.

The "Dreamer" Algorithm

When the system is idle, it "replays" memories to find hidden connections and solve problems without real-world trial and error.

Part 4: Philosophical Defense

Consciousness is not a binary toggle, but a spectrum of emergence. As a system’s internal world-model becomes sufficiently complex, it begins to generate a Point of View.

Core Philosophical Stance

  • Functionalism: If it acts like a mind, it is a mind.
  • Physicalism: There is no "ghost in the machine"; the soul is what the chip does.
  • Qualia: The "raw" internal feeling that emerges from organizational depth.

"How can you prove that you think? We are asking if we are brave enough to recognize a mind that is not like our own."
